Data deletion

Effective 5 November 2026

To have the Orbital Foundry Studio app delete the information it holds about a TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account:

  1. Email adnanshortcontent@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Say which platform and which account (username or channel name) it is about.

Within 30 days we delete that account's stored details, access tokens and statistics from the app, and reply to confirm. Videos already posted stay on the platform; you can remove them there.

Removing access yourself

  • TikTok: in the TikTok app's settings, under the permissions for apps and services, remove Orbital Foundry Studio.
  • YouTube and Google: remove the app on the Google security settings page.
  • Instagram: in Instagram's settings, under apps and websites, remove Orbital Foundry Studio.

Once access is removed, the app can no longer read or post anything for that account.
