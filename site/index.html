Orbital Foundry Studio

An independent studio making original explainer videos.

What we make

We research, write and produce our own videos: long-form explainers for YouTube, and short videos for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels. Every video is made from our own scripts, licensed or public-domain footage and music, and our own graphics. Our narration is an AI voice, and we say so in every video's description. We label AI-generated scenes as each platform requires.

Our posting app

The Orbital Foundry Studio app is a private tool that runs on our own computer. It uploads the videos we have made to our own accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram, and reads the statistics of those accounts so we can learn what our viewers like. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.

On TikTok, the app:

  • asks us to sign in with TikTok and approve its access (TikTok Login Kit);
  • uploads each finished video to our TikTok inbox as a draft (Content Posting API), where we review it and post it ourselves in the TikTok app;
  • reads our basic profile (display name and avatar) to show which account it is using.

Nothing is posted without a person approving it. How we handle the information involved is described in our Privacy Policy, and the rules for using the app are in our Terms of Service.
