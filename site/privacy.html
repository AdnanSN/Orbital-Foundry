Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Orbital Foundry Studio app (“the app”) handles, why, and how you can have it removed. “We” and “us” mean Orbital Foundry Studio, an independent studio run by one person.

What the app is

The app is a private tool that runs on our own computer. It uploads videos we have made to our own accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram, and reads the statistics of those accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access other people's accounts.

Information the app handles

  • TikTok: when the account owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives the account's identifier (open ID), display name and avatar, and access tokens. With the video.upload permission it sends our videos to that account's inbox as drafts. If we later add permission to read video statistics, it reads them only for our own videos.
  • YouTube: through YouTube API Services, the app reads our channel's identifier and title, uploads our videos with their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and caption files, and reads our channel's analytics.
  • Instagram: through Meta's Instagram API, the app reads our account's identifier and username, publishes our Reels, and reads their insights.

The app does not collect information about viewers, followers or any other users, apart from the totals each platform reports for our own content (for example views and likes). This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

How we use it

Only to upload and schedule our own videos, to show which account the app is using, and to measure how our videos perform so we can make better ones.

Where it is kept and who sees it

Everything is stored on our own computer: account details and statistics in a local database, and access tokens in a local configuration file. We do not sell or rent this information, and we do not share it with anyone, except that each request goes to the platform it belongs to (TikTok, Google or Meta).

How long we keep it

Access tokens are kept until access is revoked or expires. Statistics are kept while the studio runs its channels. Everything can be deleted on request (see below).

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access to a Google account at any time on the Google security settings page.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can remove the app's access to a TikTok or Instagram account in that app's settings, under the apps and services or apps and websites permissions. To have information the app holds deleted, follow the steps on our data deletion page.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.
